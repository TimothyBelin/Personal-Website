I am a Ph.D. student in Mechanical Engineering at the **University of California, Berkeley**, where I work with [Professor Simo Mäkiharju](https://me.berkeley.edu/people/simo-makiharju/) in the FLOW Lab.

My research focuses on experimental fluid mechanics, particularly multiphase flows, wall-bounded turbulence, and frictional drag reduction through air lubrication. My current work investigates how gas injection modifies the air-water interface and the underlying turbulent boundary layer. I use experimental flow diagnostics, including particle image velocimetry (PIV), to characterize flow structures and elucidate turbulence topology.

Prior to Berkeley, I received my B.S. in Mechanical Engineering from [Union College](https://www.union.edu/) in 2026. There, I conducted undergraduate research under [Professor Ali Hamed](https://muse.union.edu/hameda/), experimentally investigating turbulent boundary layer flows around finite wall-mounted obstacles.
