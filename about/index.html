About

I was born and raised in Lafayette, Colorado, where I attended Centaurus High School and graduated in 2021 (sko warriors!). I took a gap year away from academia to take a break from online school, working on small scale residential remodels with Carve Construction in Boulder, Colorado. Then I spent four years in Schenectady, New York, studying mechanical engineering and mathematics at Union College (sko chargers!), where I picked up ultimate frisbee. Recently, I moved out to the bay area to pursue a Ph.D. in mechanical engineering at the University of California, Berkeley.

Outside the lab, I enjoy cycling, hiking, skiing, ultimate frisbee, and exploring the outdoors. My favorite weekend activity is to grab my road bike, plan a fun route, and go exploring on new roads in my area (especially if they are very hilly).
